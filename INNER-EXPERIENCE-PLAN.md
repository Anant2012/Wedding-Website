# Anjali & Rushabh: the inner invitation

## Current content edit

The original chapter plans below document the visual foundation. The current
shorter invitation retains that artwork, palette, typography and physical opening.
The separate letter was removed with approval; its invitation wording remains in
the opening. Numbered folios, repeated introductions and decorative date repeats
are removed. The countdown and calendar download remain after discovery, without
a second date leaf. Ceremony details and practical directions are retained.
The decorative route illustration and its caption have been removed; venue names,
addresses and Google Maps direction links remain.
Spacing is rebalanced around the remaining content rather than preserving empty
chapter-height gaps.

Before the route illustration was removed, the initial content edit reduced the
390 x 844 unlocked journey from 11,095px to 7,217px (35% shorter).
The production opening and scratch journey
passed with no runtime errors or reveal-card movement. Responsive checks passed
at 320, 375, 390, 393, 412, 720, 768, 1024 and 1440px widths, plus 844 x 390
landscape, including keyboard reveal, menu focus, calendar download, map links,
pre-reveal date concealment and explicit canvas-failure recovery. Opening and
shared-palette file fingerprints remain unchanged.

The Gwalior frame now has an opt-in engraved finish: warm dimensional stone,
recessed jali windows, a fine architectural border and subtle paper grain. The
hero keeps its original fort rendering. This refinement adds no copy or section
height and does not restore the removed foreground flowers.

## Creative contract

The opening component, its stylesheet, its timing and its interaction are protected.
The existing rose palette and font families remain unchanged. No new contrasting
theme, stock illustration, copied invitation layout, fabricated event time, or
fictional geographic route will be introduced.

The story is a collection of different heirloom objects, not a collection of
identical cards. Rhythm: quiet, emotional, tactile, intimate, cinematic, festive,
romantic, grand, quiet, practical, personal.

## Audit

The current experience repeats a centered heading, small ornament, arched or
rectangular panel, faded fort and blurred text entrance. Its softness is consistent,
but that consistency removes contrast. The architecture is too schematic and too
faint to identify the city. Haldi and Sangeet are ordinary timeline cards; the
wedding is another nested card. The date and venues feel like website components.

## Complete chapter plan

### 01 / Home: A Sacred Beginning

1. **Current:** centered names inside a tall arch, crest, divider, pale fort.
2. **Problem:** everything has equal decorative weight; the names look boxed in.
3. **Concept:** a full-height editorial title page with generous names and a
   low, cropped palace/fort horizon, like an illustrated book's frontispiece.
4. **Cultural element:** small Ganesha invocation; Man Mandir-inspired horizon.
5. **Motion:** a restrained depth shift in the horizon on scroll, not another
   name entrance after the physical opening.
6. **Type:** oversized Cormorant names, a small italic ampersand, crisp date/place.
7. **Background:** warm ivory paper with one blush wash; no inset card.
8. **Foreground:** a single botanical edge, discreet personal crest.
9. **Transition:** an editorial folio leads into the family spread; no scroll snap.

### 02 / Families: Two Families. One Beautiful Beginning.

1. **Current:** two identical arched family cards separated by an ampersand.
2. **Problem:** symmetry reads as a reusable component, not two families meeting.
3. **Concept:** staggered, unboxed family portraits made of type and illustrated
   botanical margins; one translucent paper overlaps the other.
4. **Cultural element:** jasmine/lotus versus marigold; a quiet central Om.
5. **Motion:** the two papers move gently inward with scroll; the central union
   becomes the point of rest. No large rotation.
6. **Type:** large first names, smaller surnames; parents in readable serif,
   relationship labels in restrained sans-serif.
7. **Background:** cream, with a single angled vellum sheet.
8. **Foreground:** offset botanical sprays; no portraits or invented family crests.
9. **Transition:** a small A & R signature joins the two sides and leads to the
   date keepsake.

### 03 / Save the date: A Little Secret, in Gold

1. **Current:** scratch card between a small heading and help text.
2. **Problem:** an isolated widget rather than something discovered in the box.
3. **Concept:** a small foil keepsake on a wider letterpress date page, with a
   numbered folio and handwritten-feeling note.
4. **Cultural element:** one lotus impression, not a complete floral border.
5. **Motion:** preserve the actual scratch interaction and easy tap alternative.
6. **Type:** expressive short heading, clear date beneath the foil; instructions
   remain readable and actionable.
7. **Background:** quiet blush, a offset cream backing sheet.
8. **Foreground:** restrained gold foil and the existing small reveal effects.
9. **Transition:** the revealed paper visually continues into the family letter.

### 04 / The invitation: A Note from Our Hearts

1. **Current:** centered verse inside decorative vine/architectural framing.
2. **Problem:** repeats the opening title page and relies on more ornaments.
3. **Concept:** an intimate open letter on one square-edged sheet, with a generous
   inset margin and a small marginal annotation.
4. **Cultural element:** existing Devanagari blessing; small jasmine sprig.
5. **Motion:** a translucent overlay draws back a few pixels; the letter remains
   easy to read rather than appearing one line at a time.
6. **Type:** editorial serif paragraph, emphasized final phrase, script signature.
7. **Background:** ivory, gently shadowed vellum rather than an arch.
8. **Foreground:** one botanical impression and an embossed A & R corner mark.
9. **Transition:** a full-width sandstone/blush horizon replaces the paper margin.

### 05 / Gwalior: From Gwalior, with Love

1. **Current:** faint fort icon beneath centered heritage text.
2. **Problem:** a generic skyline watermark, not a memorable sense of place.
3. **Concept:** a large original panorama of a hilltop fort and palace bastions;
   text above the scene, not illegibly over its details.
4. **Cultural element:** Gwalior Fort/Man Mandir chhatris, crenellations, lattice
   windows and sandstone plateau.
5. **Motion:** scroll reveals the distant mass, then draws selected architectural
   contours. No pinned-scroll trap.
6. **Type:** oversized "Gwalior", small "From Gwalior, with love", short poetic line.
7. **Background:** blush sky, champagne sun, distant sandstone wash.
8. **Foreground:** an unobstructed fort panorama and a fine architectural caption;
   no overlaid floral spray.
9. **Transition:** the quiet sandstone landscape leads into the secret reveal.

### 06 / Haldi: A Morning in Bloom

1. **Current:** a timeline card with a small circular flower badge.
2. **Problem:** a schedule item, not a ceremony with its own atmosphere.
3. **Concept:** an asymmetric sunlit floral composition, with large marigold
   branches entering the margins and readable details in open space.
4. **Cultural element:** original marigold flower heads and leaves; turmeric
   warmth expressed in the existing champagne/gold accents.
5. **Motion:** a short floral entrance and gentle scroll depth, not continuous
   busy movement.
6. **Type:** very large italic "Haldi"; 02 December 2026 / 11:00 AM clearly grouped.
7. **Background:** pale blush and a restrained warm sunlight wash.
8. **Foreground:** detailed marigold sprays; one small ceremony number.
9. **Transition:** a scalloped paper edge and warmer blush announce evening.

### 07 / Sangeet: An Evening in Rhythm

1. **Current:** the same timeline-card system as Haldi.
2. **Problem:** changing the icon does not create an evening mood.
3. **Concept:** a palace courtyard seen through a scalloped architectural opening;
   event details above the courtyard threshold.
4. **Cultural element:** jharokhas, arcade rhythm, hanging lamps and musical curves.
5. **Motion:** courtyard layers reveal at different rates; a few small lights
   breathe, stopping when off-screen and in reduced motion.
6. **Type:** elegant large roman title, italic supporting line, clear time/venue.
7. **Background:** slightly deeper dusty blush, never black, purple or nightclub gold.
8. **Foreground:** restrained hanging lamps and two botanical details.
9. **Transition:** a quiet "The next day" interlude clears the visual field.

### 08 / Wedding: Before Fire & Family

1. **Current:** darkish inset arched wedding card, small garland and diya icons.
2. **Problem:** insufficient scale or distinction for the ceremonial climax.
3. **Concept:** a full-width original mandap: pillars, floral canopy, hanging
   jasmine, steps, lotus and lamps; no people or generic stock scene.
4. **Cultural element:** sacred fire/diya language and floral mandap architecture;
   retain the existing auspicious Devanagari text.
5. **Motion:** tiny light points, flowers, pillars, canopy, garlands and diya warmth
   build in a single approximately two-second sequence. Date/title follow the
   architecture without withholding essential details for a long animation.
6. **Type:** "The Wedding" as the largest ceremony title; 3 December 2026 and
   Abhinanadan Vatika in high-contrast type with no text over intricate linework.
7. **Background:** luminous ivory and blush, a very faint geometric halo.
8. **Foreground:** floral canopy and lotus/diya clusters; limited petal density.
9. **Transition:** the scene resolves into a quiet, generous calendar page.

### 09 / Countdown: Until Our Forever

1. **Current:** heading, date and four numbers in a mostly empty screen.
2. **Problem:** looks like a generic website timer and loses momentum.
3. **Concept:** editorial calendar leaf with an oversized 03, December 2026 and
   the live countdown as a small typographic baseline beneath it.
4. **Cultural element:** a small embossed lotus/crest; architecture rests here.
5. **Motion:** one gentle paper entrance; keep the real second-by-second timer,
   without rolling digit effects or screen-reader announcements every second.
6. **Type:** large serif day, restrained month/year, stable tabular timer numerals.
7. **Background:** cream with a softly offset vellum sheet.
8. **Foreground:** a thin gold rule and optional local calendar download.
9. **Transition:** a drawn route line introduces the practical venue guide.

### 10 / Venues: A Celebration in Gwalior

1. **Current:** two similar venue cards with "View Location" links.
2. **Problem:** generic and disconnected from the city's visual language.
3. **Concept:** an illustrated, explicitly not-to-scale journey between two
   numbered venue entries, followed by obvious direction links.
4. **Cultural element:** small architectural vignettes, trees and a fort horizon.
5. **Motion:** the decorative connecting route draws once on entry.
6. **Type:** large venue names, ceremony labels and full addresses; "Get directions"
   at least a 44px tap target, never hidden behind an artistic interaction.
7. **Background:** warm ivory, a faint map-paper texture, no live maps embed.
8. **Foreground:** two numbered markers; no fabricated distances or travel times.
9. **Transition:** the route ends with a personal signature, not another map card.

### 11 / Closing: With Love

1. **Current:** names, divider, text and diya within another full-screen chapter.
2. **Problem:** pleasant, but too similar to earlier pages.
3. **Concept:** an intimate handwritten-feeling sign-off with a large embossed
   personal monogram and one low floral arrangement.
4. **Cultural element:** a single diya and existing auspicious closing.
5. **Motion:** one restrained petal increase and a soft floral settling motion.
6. **Type:** script "With love", readable couple names, short warm message.
7. **Background:** quiet blush paper with generous empty space.
8. **Foreground:** a panoramic lotus garden, personal A & R crest in a new position.
9. **Transition:** a clean end, date/place imprint and discreet return-to-top link.

## Delivery record

### Current flow: a secret until discovered

The secret-first flow supersedes the earlier chapter order and automatic
reduced-motion date reveal described in the original delivery record below.

1. Date-free physical opening and hero, with an animated scroll-discovery cue.
2. Two families.
3. Gwalior heritage.
4. The secret: scratch the foil or use the keyboard/tap alternative.
5. Only after completion: countdown, invitation letter, Haldi, Sangeet, wedding,
   directions and final blessing.

The application owns one reveal state. Later chapters are not mounted before
completion, rather than merely being covered or faded out. Navigation exposes
only available chapters, and the calendar download is linked only after reveal.
Reloading begins a new sealed visit; scrolling back during a visit retains the
revealed state. This is presentation sequencing, not access control over client
source code or a separately requested calendar file.

The date beneath the foil is hidden on first paint and excluded from the
accessibility tree until completion. Partial scratching reveals only that paper
surface; clearing at least 45% unlocks the subsequent chapters. Missing canvas
support or reduced motion never reveals the secret automatically: the explicit
tap/keyboard alternative remains available.

The approved opening's animation, timings, materials and geometry are retained.
Only its three date-bearing text nodes are replaced. Metadata remains date-free.
The disclosure uses restrained dust, eight petals, a warmer paper wash, then an
invitation to continue; there is no automatic jump away from the discovered date.

The scroll cue fits inside the first viewport, has a safe lower margin, and
disappears after the first 24px of scrolling. Its loop and all reveal motion are
disabled for reduced motion without changing access to the content.

Cleanup removes the hidden palette-switch control, the scratch card's redundant
entrance observer and auto-reveal machinery, invisible countdown separators and
unused delay attributes. Intro and celebration motion have separate lifecycles,
so unlocking does not replay already-read chapters.
